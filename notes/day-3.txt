directives:
-----------------
a. attribute directive:
------------------------------ 
ngClass, ngStyle, formGroup, formElement etc.
use this as custom attribute
to extend the behavior of existing elements
they are NOT used with any special character in the template

b. structural directive
------------------------------
*ngIf, *ngIf, *ngFor, *ngSwitch
use this as custom attribute
manipulate DOM
they are used with * in the template

are being replaced with @if, @else, @for, @switch etc.

c. componeht directive or Component
------------------------------------------
use this as custom HTML tag
decorated with @Component() decorator

@Directive({
	selector:'[ngClass]'
})
export class NgClass{
	
}
